## 📌 Deliverables Summary

1. **Digital Customer QR Menu (`/`)**:
   - Progressive Web App (PWA) with instant loading (< 0.5s).
   - Dual-Ambiance Switcher: **Midnight Roastery** (Teal) and **Roastery Cream** (Ivory parchment).
   - Two-Tier Craft Hierarchy: **COFFEE & BEVERAGES** and **BAKERY & BITES**.
   - Complimentary Guest Wi-Fi details with 1-click clipboard copy.

2. **Master Roastery QR Stand Generator**:
   - 4x6" acrylic table tent card generation in both Cream and Midnight Teal styles.
   - Warm cream background (`#E5CFAF`) with deep teal QR pattern (`#0E2F2C`).

3. **Management & Admin Portal (`/admin`)**:
   - Menu items and live pricing editor (ETB).
   - 1-click sold-out visibility toggle.
   - Live CMS for hero headlines, Wi-Fi passwords, and roastery craft manifesto.
   - Real-time customer analytics and popularity rankings.

4. **Deployment & Source Code**:
   - Docker containerization, Render blueprint (`render.yaml`), and local Windows startup script (`start-kabun.bat`).
   - Clean, modular TypeScript architecture.
